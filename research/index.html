Research

My research has been in applied computer vision — measuring physical things from images under conditions that refuse to cooperate. It is also where I learned that the model is seldom the part that decides whether a system works.

Publications

  1. Dynamic Image Analysis of Microscopic Particles

    D K Bharath Reddy, et al. · Springer CCIS — MIND Conference · 2024

    Follow-up research extending dynamic image-analysis techniques to the evaluation of microscopic particles in motion.

  2. Micro-Particle Anomaly Detection & Shape Classification

    D K Bharath Reddy, et al. · IEEE Conference, Vellore · 2023

    Foundational work applying computer-vision techniques to identify anomalies, analyse shape, and classify micro-sized particles.

  3. Web-Based Application for Healthy Lifestyle

    D K Bharath Reddy, et al. · 12th Project Innovation Contest (PIC), KIIT Odisha — pp. 49–60 · 2023

    A digital health platform combining ML food classification, QR-based record sharing, and ESP8266 IoT hardware.

Interests

I am most interested in the infrastructure that intelligent systems depend on rather than the models themselves: information retrieval over large and changing datasets, the interfaces through which models are permitted to query enterprise data, and how any of this is evaluated once it leaves a controlled environment.
